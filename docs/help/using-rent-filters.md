# Finding a place on Rent

Open **/rent** to browse listings.

## Location is optional

Click **Use my location** to share your location. Click **Clear location** to remove it. If you prefer, skip location and pick a destination instead.

**Nearest only** sorts by distance. It is available only when your shared location is inside Costa Rica. Outside Costa Rica, sorting uses **Relevance**.

## Destinations

Choose a destination from the list, or choose **All of Costa Rica** to see every listing.

## Category chips

Tap a category chip to narrow the listings to that type of place.

## Filters

Click **Filters** to set:

- a minimum and maximum price;
- the number of bedrooms.

## Sort

Choose one of these from **Sort**:

- **Relevance**
- **Price: Low to High**
- **Price: High to Low**
- **Nearest only** (needs a shared location inside Costa Rica)

## Reset

Click **Reset** to clear your destination, chips, filters, and sort.

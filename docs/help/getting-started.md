# Getting started with SafeTrust

SafeTrust lets you rent a place and pay through an escrow. The payment is held on the Stellar network and released once the stay is confirmed.

## Without an account

You can browse listings on **/rent**, use the filters, and read these guides. Nothing you do while browsing is saved.

## When you need an account

You need an account to book a stay or to list a place as a host.

## When you need a wallet

You need a Stellar wallet to pay. Connect it when the app asks you to pay. A wallet is not needed for browsing.

Demo: some listings and actions use demo data. Demo actions are not saved. Tracked in [issue #696](https://github.com/safetrustcr/frontend-SafeTrust/issues/696).
